From grove to pouch

Four things happen.
That's the whole recipe.

01

The banana

Raw Nendran, the long Kerala banana with the firm, starchy flesh that turns crisp instead of soggy. Picked green, never ripe.

02

The slice

Peeled and sliced paper-thin, straight into the oil. Thin enough to shatter, thick enough to hold the crunch.

03

The coconut oil

Fried in pure coconut oil in a wide uruli, with turmeric water for that deep gold. This is the smell of every Kerala bakery.

04

The seal

A pinch of salt, cooled, and packed. That's it: four ingredients, and you can pronounce all of them.
