Cooked with love

Varthaai was born from a cherished childhood memory—the golden afternoon light filtering through kitchen windows as grandmother transformed raw bananas into crisp, delectable "varutha kaaya" or banana chips. More than just a snack, these moments represented love, tradition, and the simple joy of something made with care. Today, we've preserved this legacy while elevating it to extraordinary heights, creating premium banana chips that honor Kerala's culinary heritage with uncompromising quality.

In a world of mass production, we stand apart—selecting single-origin bananas at perfect ripeness, sliced into finest chips, fried with turmeric powder, coconut oil and our secret ingredient "love". It's the golden token of love. You deserve nothing less than the best in your golden moments and it's our promise.
